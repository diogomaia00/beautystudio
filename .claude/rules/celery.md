# CELERY REQUIREMENTS

## Use cases:

### Messages
#### appointment reminders
Send a message via email or sms (in the prefered client via) to the client reminding them of the appointment 
- The message must include the day and hour of the appointment, the type of service, service value

#### birthday message
Send a sms to the client wishing the wonderful birthday in name of the Beauty Studio team

### Reports
#### monthly report generation
In the first day of the month a report about the previous month must be presented to the staff member in the BO with the following information:
    - Number of hours worked
    - Number of appointments booked, made, canceled
    - Number of different clients received and new clients
    - Top 3 most popular services
    - Revenue made in euros (total and per service type)
    - Revenue made per hour worked
